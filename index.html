Why we exist

For a lot of people in Nigeria, birthdays pass quietly. No calls. No cake. Nobody to say the words. If your family isn't close by, or there's no money to celebrate, the day comes and goes like any other.

BirthdayNaija exists so that doesn't happen, because every birthday and every life matters. We put your name on our Birthday Wall, send you a birthday shoutout, and make sure someone celebrates you, every single year.
